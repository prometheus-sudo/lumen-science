# Mechanics

**Field:** physics · **Module:** Classical Physics · **Concept id:** phys-mech

---

Mechanics describes the motion of material bodies and the forces that change that motion. Newton’s laws provide the classical core: inertia, force as rate of change of momentum, and equal-and-opposite interaction pairs. Work and energy rephrase the same physics; momentum conservation is central to collisions. Free-body diagrams and consistent units turn the narrative into solvable problems.
