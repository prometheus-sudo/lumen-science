# Characteristics of life

**Field:** biology · **Module:** Foundations of Biology · **Concept id:** bio-life

---

Biology begins with a deceptively simple question: what counts as living? The standard answer is a cluster of properties rather than a single essence. Living systems are organised into cells or systems built by cells; they transform energy and matter through metabolism; they maintain internal conditions within workable bounds; they grow and reproduce; they respond to their surroundings; and populations of them change across generations by evolution.

Viruses complicate the boundary. They possess genetic material and evolve, yet lack independent metabolism and cellular structure. Organisation is hierarchical from molecules to ecosystems, with emergent properties at each level.
