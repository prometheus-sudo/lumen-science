# Chemical bonding

**Field:** chemistry · **Module:** General Chemistry · **Concept id:** chem-bond

---

Chemical bonds hold atoms in molecules and lattices. Ionic bonding involves electron transfer and electrostatic attraction; covalent bonding shares electron pairs; metallic bonding involves delocalised electrons. Polarity, shape and bond strength connect electronic structure to macroscopic properties. Continuous chemical reasoning always asks what electrons are doing and what energy changes accompany bond formation and breaking.
