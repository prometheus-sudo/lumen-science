# Population growth

**Field:** ecology · **Module:** Population Ecology · **Concept id:** eco-growth

---

A population is a group of individuals of one species in a defined area. Change equals births minus deaths plus immigration minus emigration. From that identity come models of unbounded and density-limited growth.

## Exponential and logistic ideas

Constant per-capita rates yield exponential change—a baseline, not a long-term forecast. As density rises, per-capita growth often falls toward a carrying capacity that itself shifts with resources and climate. Age structure and chance events matter, especially in small populations.

## Application

Abundance is estimated with counts, mark-recapture and remote methods. Fisheries and pest management depend on whether removals add to natural mortality and whether populations compensate.
