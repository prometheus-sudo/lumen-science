# Community structure and succession

**Field:** ecology · **Module:** Community Ecology · **Concept id:** eco-rel

---

Community structure is the set of species, abundances and organisation in a place. Succession is directional change after disturbance or on new ground. Primary succession starts without developed soil; secondary succession follows disturbance that leaves soil and propagules. Facilitation, inhibition and tolerance describe how early species affect later ones. Climaxes are no longer treated as single fixed endpoints in every landscape.
