# Species interactions

**Field:** ecology · **Module:** Population Ecology · **Concept id:** eco-int

---

Species interact through feeding, competition, mutual benefit and harm. Ecologists classify interactions by fitness effects on each partner. Exploitation (predation, herbivory, parasitism) transfers energy and shapes defences. Mutualism benefits both partners; facilitation can stabilise communities. Interaction signs and strengths change with environment, so field experiments remain essential.
