# Carbon cycles and ecosystems

**Field:** ecology · **Module:** Climate Ecology · **Concept id:** eco-carbon

---

Carbon is both a nutrient and a climate-active element. Photosynthesis, respiration and decomposition move carbon among atmosphere, organisms and soils. Ecosystem sinks and sources shift with disturbance. Protecting high-carbon habitats is a mitigation tool that still requires cuts in fossil emissions at source.
