# Biodiversity

**Field:** ecology · **Module:** Community Ecology · **Concept id:** eco-biodiv

---

Biodiversity is variety at genetic, species and ecosystem levels. Richness and evenness describe species diversity; sampling effort affects what is observed. Diverse communities can show more stable function in some settings. Habitat loss, overharvest, invasives, pollution and climate change drive declines, often together. Conservation acts on those drivers and on remaining populations and places.
