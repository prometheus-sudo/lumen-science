# Competition

**Field:** ecology · **Module:** Population Ecology · **Concept id:** eco-comp

---

Competition is a mutually negative interaction over limited shared resources such as light, water, nutrients, space or mates. Intraspecific competition is often strongest; interspecific competition can exclude a species or allow coexistence when niches, disturbance or predators prevent monopoly. Competitive exclusion is a hypothesis about identical limiting requirements, not a verdict on every pair of species.
